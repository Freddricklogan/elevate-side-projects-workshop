Facilitator guide

Run of show, 45 minutes

This is a working session, not a lecture. Every block ends with students doing something on the handout. Put slides.html full screen on the projector and press P to open the presenter view on your laptop screen: notes, next slide, clock, run of show, and the student question board.

TimeBlockWhat you doAsk the room
12:30DoorsProjector on slide 1. Handout link and check-in QR on screen. Greet; ask each arriving student what they'd build if nobody graded it."What would you build if nobody graded it?"
0:00The ideaSlide 2. Start the 60-second timer. Students write one idea. Poll Q1 on phones."Stuck? What annoyed you this week? That's a project."
0:03Why this worksSlides 3–4. Roadmap in 30 seconds. 52% of Gen Z freelanced (Upwork, 2023). Six-week cycle (Shape Up): fix the time, flex the scope."What's the smallest version someone else could use tomorrow?"
0:10Two sentences + scope shapeSlides 5–7. Two minutes of silent drafting (Step 2 on handout). Volunteer reads theirs; type it into the builder. Cut one idea to a slice out loud. Click the tab for majors in the room."Give me an idea. What's version two?" (repeat until one thing is left)
0:18Three routesSlide 8. Run the dropdown with two students' answers. Say the IRS $400 line plainly; name Kaplan and the Idea Shop. Poll Q2."Recruiter, client, or judge: who's your audience?"
0:26The Illinois Tech map + ideasSlides 9–10. Five pathways. Name the Elevate pieces that count: hackathons, AWS Jam, Forage, the venture elective."Name one thing that annoyed you this semester you could fix in four weeks."
0:33Scope one projectSlide 11. Start the 8-minute timer. Walk the room; push for a Size number and a Publish date. At 8:00 start the 3-minute swap."Partner: what would you cut to ship in half the time?"
0:41Checklist + check-inSlides 12–13. Read the checklist fast. Leave the QR up. Students download or email the worksheet and book a one-on-one."What's your first step before Friday?"

If the room goes quiet

  • Scope one of your own ideas on screen; ask the room only for what to cut.
  • Ask for a show of hands on the routes slide; tap the tally so the screen moves.
  • Use the field tabs: "Who's in engineering? Here's yours."

Bring

  • Laptop and adapter, this site loaded (works offline once opened), handout link on the board.
  • Printed handouts for students without a device (handout.html prints cleanly).
  • Kaplan Startup Studio and Idea Shop pages open in tabs to show live.
  • Sign-in: the 312 QR on slide 13 records attendance for Elevate credit.

After

  • Record attendance from 312 and log the session for Elevate workshop credit.
  • Reply to any emailed worksheets within two business days with one revision note each.
  • Export the Forms responses; clear the tallies on the poll page before the next session.

Edit the site

All dates, links, and embeds live in assets/config.js. Slide text is in slides.html; each slide is one <section class="slide">. Speaker notes are the NOTES array at the bottom of that file.
