0:13 · The scope shape

Appetite. Slice. Ship.

A
Appetite. How much time you will spend, fixed in advance: hours a week times weeks. The scope bends to fit it, never the reverse.
S
Slice. The thinnest version that works end to end. One feature, one page, one chair, one client. Everything else is version two.
S
Ship. Public, by a date: a repo with a README, a post, a demo video, a listing. If nobody can find it, it did not happen.
