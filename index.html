What you'll walk out able to do

  1. Scope a side project with a fixed appetite, a thinnest working slice, and a public ship date.
  2. Write the two-sentence description that goes at the top of the repo, the post, or the gig listing.
  3. Explain how a side project becomes a freelance gig, and what a first gig needs: scope, price, contract, invoice.
  4. Map the Illinois Tech pathways: Elevate pathway courses, IPRO, Kaplan Startup Studio, Idea Shop, competitions.
  5. Date the first step, this week, and know where the finished thing gets published.
