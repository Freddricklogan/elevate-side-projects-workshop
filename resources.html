Where the project can go

The project is the same in every route. Only the audience changes: a recruiter, a client, or a judge.

Sell it: freelance gigs

The side project is your sample

  • Contra (commission-free for freelancers) · Getting started on Upwork · Fiverr seller setup.

The scope template (copy it)

Project: [name] For: [who uses it or pays for it] Size: I will spend [hours] hours a week for [weeks] weeks. Hone: Version one is only [the thinnest version that works end to end]. Not now: [everything else] Invite: One person who will try it: [name]. Publish: I will publish it on [GitHub Pages / Google Sites / Canva / Behance / Notion / a LinkedIn post] by [date]. First step: [day this week]: [the step]

No code? Publish on Google Sites, Canva, Behance, Notion or a LinkedIn post instead.

First-gig checklist

PieceWhat it says
ScopeOne paragraph: what you will deliver, what you will not, how many revisions
PriceFixed for the first gig. Rate guide: target hourly plus a 25–50% markup for your own costs
AgreementWritten, even an email: scope, price, dates, cancellation, who owns the work
InvoiceDate, description, amount, due date, how to pay. Half up front is normal
TaxesTrack income; $400+ net self-employment income goes on a tax return
